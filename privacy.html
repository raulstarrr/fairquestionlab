Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

"Fair Question Lab Tools" (the YouTube and Google integration) and "Fair Question Lab Uploader" (the TikTok integration) are private tools used only by the owner of the Fair Question Lab channels. They upload our own videos to our own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts and read the statistics of those same accounts.

What we access

  • Only the accounts owned by Fair Question Lab, after the owner signs in and approves access.
  • The permissions used: uploading videos and setting thumbnails, reading basic profile information, and reading channel and video statistics.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or process data about other people or viewers.
  • We don't sell or share any data with third parties.
  • We don't post without the owner's review.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder, and are never published. Access can be revoked at any time in the Google account permissions page or in TikTok's settings.
