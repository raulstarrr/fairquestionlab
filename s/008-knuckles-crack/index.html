The short answer

It isn't a bubble bursting. In 2015 researchers filmed one volunteer's finger joints in real-time MRI: pulling the joint apart drops the pressure in the joint fluid, the surfaces cling and then snap apart, and a gas pocket forms. That's the crack. The pocket was still there after the sound, and the same joint couldn't crack again for about 20 minutes.
