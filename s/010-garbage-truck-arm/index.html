The short answer

One driver, one joystick and a robot arm. On one popular automated side-loader the arm reaches up to 9 feet, grips the cart, tips it into the hopper and sets it back down, and the maker says the whole cycle takes 8 seconds. That's why many cities ask for carts 3 feet apart with the arrow on the lid facing the street.
