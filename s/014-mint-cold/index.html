The short answer

Menthol switches on TRPM8, your cold sensor, which normally fires below about 25 °C. So your nerves report cold at 36 °C. Chillies do the same with TRPV1, your heat sensor. David Julius and Ardem Patapoutian shared the 2021 Nobel Prize for finding these sensors.
